Alpha 0.2.0 · for the PreenFM3

A new way to make music with your PreenFM3.

Chimera is alternative firmware. It gives your synth new sound engines, effects and sequencing tools, and a screen that shows what each module does to the sound.

Download latest How to install

Runs on the original hardware: no modification, nothing else to buy. You can switch back to the stock PreenFM3 firmware at any time. Chimera is still in development, so expect rough edges.

What is in it

Engines

ALGO six-operator phase-modulation synth. MODAL struck, plucked and bowed resonators. VA two variable-wave oscillators combined by MODE (sync, ring, FM, phase and more), with a SUB, a NOISE source and STABILITY drift. TAPE plays sources from the card through four machines: WALK, STRETCH, SCRUB and WAVE. DRUM is marked COMING SOON.

Multitimbral

Every Part runs its own engine, with its own mixer strip, output pair and MIDI control.

Chain navigation

Each Part is a chain of pages. MINUS and PLUS move along it, and the same keys work everywhere.

It shows you

A focus band names the last thing you touched, with a picture of its value. The filter is drawn from its real response.

ORBIT

A ring sequencer, one hold of SEQ away.

Modulation and effects

A MOD matrix, five filters (CLEAN, 2040, DIRTY, LADDER and SEM) with drive, fuzz or fold, a low-pass gate, and chorus, delay and reverb, each with its own animated picture.

Install in brief

  1. Check the bootloader. Hold MENU while powering on. It should say pfm3 BootLoader 1.09.
  2. Download CHIMERA.BIN, CHIMERA.BK2 and factory-v1.zip from the latest release. The zip holds 128 factory Sounds, the sources the TAPE Sounds play and four demo projects.
  3. Copy them to the SD card: both firmware files to its top, the unzipped CHIMERA folder beside them. Pick CHIMERA.BIN in the bootloader and press button 1. Chimera installs the second file on its first start.
