Chimera FAQ

Short answers for alpha testers. New to the controls? Read the quick start first. Labels below are as they appear on screen.

Contents: Sounds · Projects · Modulation · Engines · TAPE · MIDI and playing · Effects · The screen · Firmware and bugs

Sounds

How do I load a Sound? Hold EDIT and press Bn to open the Sound browser for Part n. Turn encoder 1 to the Sound and press EDIT. Turn encoder 2 to switch between the PROJECT tab (the project's own Sounds), the LIBRARY tab (Sounds saved on the card) and the FACTORY tab (the factory Sounds). If the Part has edits you haven't saved, it asks first.

How do I start a new Sound from INIT? In the Sound browser, turn encoder 1 up past Sound 01 to the INIT rows (INIT ALGO, INIT MODAL, INIT VA, INIT TAPE) and press EDIT. Or, to reset the Part to INIT of its engine: SETTINGS › PART › CLEAR.

How do I save a Sound? In the browser's PROJECT tab, press MIX + EDIT: it saves the Part's Sound over its own slot, or to a new slot if it has none. The hint shows how much memory it takes. SETTINGS › PART › SAVE TO PROJ gives you the choice (OVER SLOT, TO NEW SLOT). Sounds saved this way live in the project, so then hold MENU to save the project to the card.

How do I keep a Sound for other projects? SETTINGS › PART › SAVE TO LIBRARY, or MIX + EDIT on the browser's LIBRARY tab. It asks for a name and writes the Sound to the card.

How do I clear a Sound slot? In the browser's PROJECT tab, select it and press MIX + MINUS. It asks before clearing.

Where are the factory Sounds? Unzip factory-v1.zip from the releases page and copy what's in its CHIMERA folder onto the card over USB (hold MENU at power-on, then button 3), as in the install guide. It holds the Sounds, the sources the TAPE Sounds play and four demo projects. Then EDIT + B1 and turn encoder 2 to the FACTORY tab. Its 127 Sounds (ALGO, MODAL, VA and TAPE) fill three pages: turn encoder 1 down to NEXT › and press EDIT for the next, and ‹ PREVIOUS at the top goes back. Every new project starts with the factory Sounds in its PROJECT tab: TX BASS, TX EPIANO, TX BRASS, TX BELL, SAW LEAD, SQR BASS, MORPH PAD, MORPH KEYS, and the formant Sounds CHOIR, TALK BASS, BREATH and VOX. Parts 1 to 5 start on some of them. To get them back, start a new project: SETTINGS › PROJECT › LOAD PROJECT › + CREATE NEW.

How do I add Sounds from my computer? Use SD card access (hold MENU at power-on, then button 3) and copy the Sound files into the card's CHIMERA/SOUNDS folder. They show up on the LIBRARY tab.

How do I rename a Sound, or reload it from the project? SETTINGS › PART: RENAME, CLEAR, SAVE TO PROJ, RELOAD FROM PROJ, LIVE SNAP, SAVE TO LIBRARY.

Projects

How do I save the project? Hold MENU, from anywhere. A new project asks for a name first. SETTINGS › PROJECT › SAVE PROJECT AS saves a copy under a new name.

How do I load one, or start a new one? SETTINGS › PROJECT › LOAD PROJECT, select it, EDIT. The + CREATE NEW row starts a fresh project. If the current one has unsaved changes, it asks first.

How do I rename, copy or delete a project? SETTINGS › PROJECT › MANAGE PROJECTS. Select a project, then EDIT for its commands: LOAD FROM, SAVE TO, RENAME, CLEAR, DELETE, PROTECT.

Where do projects live? On the SD card, in /CHIMERA/PROJECTS/. Library Sounds are in /CHIMERA/SOUNDS/. Chimera reads and writes only inside /CHIMERA/; your stock PreenFM3 files are left alone.

How do I name things? On the naming screen: encoder 1 moves the cursor, 2 picks a letter, 3 a digit, space or dash, 4 switches case, 5 deletes. EDIT saves, MENU or SEQ cancels.

Modulation

How do I route an LFO or an envelope to a parameter?

  1. On any page, touch the parameter (turn its encoder a little) and press MIX + PLUS. The focus band says ADDED: it is now a column in the matrix.
  2. Go to the Part's MOD page (PLUS along the chain). Its first page is the matrix.
  3. Encoder 1 moves the cursor to a source row (ENV1–3, LFO1–3, VELO, NOTE), encoder 2 to the column, and encoder 5 sets the amount. Under the grid, the route reads in words, with what the amount means (for example +42 = +3.3 oct).

Other messages you may see after MIX + PLUS: ALREADY ROUTED, NOT MODULATABLE, MATRIX FULL.

How do I remove a route? On the matrix, put the cursor on it and press MIX + MINUS. On a parameter page, MIX + MINUS takes the touched parameter out of the matrix altogether.

Where are the envelopes and LFOs? Under MOD: press SEQ to step on from the matrix to ENV1, ENV2, ENV3 (envelopes), SPEED (their speeds), LFO1, LFO2, LFO3. Each LFO page draws the LFO itself, one cycle at the values you set.

What about FREQ MOD and RES MOD on the filter? They are routes themselves. On FLT › JACKS, FREQ SRC and RES SRC pick the sources; on FLT › MODULATION, FREQ MOD and RES MOD set how much. The focus band names the source (FREQ MOD LFO1). MIX + MINUS on an amount deletes the route.

Engines

How do I change a Part's engine? Load a Sound with another engine: the INIT row of that engine is a clean start. The Part's pages change to match.

What do the engines do?

EngineWhat it isIts chain
ALGOSix-operator phase modulation (FM). Two algorithms, ALG A and ALG B, with MORPH blending between themALG · OSC · FLT · AMP · MOD
MODALPhysical models: struck, plucked and bowed resonators. MODEL picks STRING, BANK, BOWED or SYMP (sympathetic strings)EXC · RES · FLT · AMP · MOD
VATwo variable-wave oscillators combined by MODE, with a SUB, a NOISE source and STABILITY driftVA · FLT · AMP · MOD
TAPEPlays sound sources from the card through a machine: WALK, STRETCH, SCRUB or WAVESRC · DECK · FLT · AMP · MOD

What do VA's MODEs do? MODE picks how OSC 1 and OSC 2 combine, and BLEND sets how far. The words at BLEND's two ends say what you get, and MODE's glyph draws the result. The MODE cell reads SOFT for SOFT SYNC; the focus band spells every MODE in full.

MODEWhat it doesBLEND from … to …
MIXThe two oscillators mixed1 · 2
SYNCOSC 2 restarts at each of OSC 1's cycles (hard sync)1 · 2
SOFT SYNCOSC 2 turns round at each of OSC 1's cycles instead of restarting. Softer than SYNC with sine and triangle waves1 · 2
RINGRing modulation: the two multipliedMIX · RING
AMOSC 2 swells and dips OSC 1's level without turning it upside down; at audio rates it adds sidebands and keeps OSC 1's note. In the top third of BLEND it runs on into RINGCLEAN · RING
FMOSC 2 bends OSC 1's speed, deep enough to run it backwards (through-zero FM). You hear OSC 1; OSC 2 is the modulatorCLEAN · DEEP
PHASEOSC 2 bends OSC 1's phase, as in phase distortionCLEAN · WARP
LOOPFMEach oscillator modulates its own frequency; BLEND also brings OSC 2 in. WAVE sets how much each one loops1 · LOOP
MAXThe louder of the two waves at each moment, its corners roundedMIX · MAX
XORThe two waves' samples combined bit by bit (exclusive or): harsh and digitalMIX · XOR
BITSThe mix crushed from 16 bits down to 2CLEAN · CRUSH

What does VA's NOISE page do? NOISE is the voice's own noise source, mixed in beside SUB after MODE and before the filter. KIND picks its colour and LEVEL how much: the lower half of LEVEL is a breath, and at full the noise is about as loud as INIT's two oscillators, never louder. As with SUB, the oscillators dip a little as LEVEL comes up, so the filter sees the same level. See the quick start for the page.

KINDSound
WHITEEven hiss across the whole range
PINKSofter, fuller hiss: less top
REDDark rumble and wind: mostly low end
DIGITALStepped, gritty noise that follows the key: brighter on higher notes

LEVEL can be modulated in the MOD matrix and learned to a MIDI CC like SUB.

What is STABILITY? On the VA page, how far each voice's oscillators drift; its glyph is a spirit level, the bubble further from the middle the more they drift. The 2040, LADDER and SEM filters have their own STABILITY, on FLT › FX: each voice gets a slightly different chip.

Why does DRUM say COMING SOON? It isn't in this release. EDIT on its row only shows COMING SOON.

What filter types are there? On the FLT page, TYPE: CLEAN, 2040, DIRTY, LADDER and SEM. The cells change with the type, and the picture is drawn from the filter's real response. Every TYPE has FEEDBACK (FDBK, on FLT › MODULATION), and every voice has an FX slot before or after the filter. See the quick start for a tour of the FILTER pages.

How do I drive or fold the sound? With the filter's FX slot. On FLT, turn FX to SOFT (drive), FUZZ or FOLD, and FX PLACE to PRE (before the filter) or POST (after it). FLT › FX holds INPUT, the effect's three pots and MIX. In the MOD matrix the FX column moves the effect's first pot (DRIVE, SUSTAIN or FOLD).

What do LPG, DECAY and STRIKE on AMP do? They turn the VCA into a low-pass gate, the kind modelled on Mutable Instruments Plaits. AMP's cells are VEL · LPG · DECAY · STRIKE. With all three at 0 the VCA is a plain VCA.

CellWhat it does
LPGDarkening follows the gain: the quieter the note, the darker it is. At full it is Plaits' low-pass gate
DECAYHow slowly the gate closes after its level falls: fast while bright, slower as it dims, like a vactrol
STRIKEEach note-on strikes the gate, as hard as the note is played, and it rings out through DECAY. It adds to the envelope routed into VCA as an accent, and a held note falls back to the envelope. With nothing routed into VCA, the strike alone plays the note, which ends when the gate shuts, even with the key held

Any filter TYPE plays in front of the gate. A sine through FLT › FX FOLD with LPG and STRIKE at full is a bongo. All three knobs can be modulated in the MOD matrix and learned to a MIDI CC.

TAPE

What is TAPE? An engine that plays sound sources: recordings on the card, held in the project's memory or streamed from the card while they play. A machine on DECK decides how a source is played:

MODELWhat it does
WALKPlays the window once through (FWD or REVERSE), round a loop (LOOP), or there and back (PING). With TRON on, each key gets its own Mellotron-style tape, 0.25 to 8 s long
STRETCHPlays the source at the key's pitch, stretched or squeezed in time: CLASSIC splices it, GRAIN plays it in grains
SCRUBMoves through the source by hand, like a tape reel under your finger: FINGER is a jog wheel, and keys can place the finger
WAVEPlays a wavetable source: FRAME, SCAN and RANGE move through its frames

The quick start has every TAPE page, annotated.

How do I play a TAPE Sound? Load one from the FACTORY tab (the Sounds tagged TAPE), or load INIT TAPE and pick a source on SRC. INIT TAPE has no source, so it is silent until you pick one.

Where are the sources? In the project, and on the card in /CHIMERA/TAPES. The factory set copies its sources there. On SRC, turn SOURCE: first come the sources the project holds, then the card's. On a card source, the focus band shows its name and size; press MIX + PLUS to load it.

Can I use my own recordings? TAPE plays Chimera's own source files (.SRC). A way to make them from your own recordings isn't part of this release.

What does STREAMS mean? A source too long for the project's memory plays from the card as it goes. SOURCE's readout then says how many notes of it can stream at once, for example 24K · 30.0 S · 7 STREAMS. REVERSE, PING, STRETCH, SCRUB and WAVE need the whole source in memory: on a streamed source they are dimmed and say … NEEDS RAM · STREAMED.

What do TAPE, AGE and WOW do? They are the tape's wear, on every machine's own page. TAPE sets how much the pitch wobbles. WOW picks how: CAPSTAN, a tape machine's steady wow and flutter, or WARBLE, a pitch that wanders. AGE darkens the sound and adds hiss; past halfway, the level drops out now and then, as worn tape does.

Why is a TAPE cell dimmed? The current setting doesn't use it, and the focus band says why: on WALK, XFADE is heard only on LOOP and with TRON, RETRIG only once through with TRON off; on STRETCH, each TYPE's cells are dimmed under the other (GRAIN TYPE ONLY, CLASSIC TYPE ONLY).

How do I free the memory sources take? SETTINGS › PROJECT › PURGE UNUSED SRC frees the memory of sources no Part uses. The files on the card stay.

MIDI and playing

How do I set a Part's MIDI channel? The CH cell on the mixer's PART page (MIX + Bn, MINUS to PRT), or all six at once at SETTINGS › MIDI CONFIG › CHANNELS. Out of the box, Part n listens on channel n.

MONO or POLY? MODE on the same PART page.

How do I set glide? On the engine's PITCH sub-page (the last in the row under ALG, RES, VA or DECK): set STEAL to GLIDE and TIME to the glide time (150 ms at INIT). A note that takes over a voice of the same Part glides from that voice's pitch; on a MONO Part, legato playing glides.

How do I set the pitch-bend range? BEND on the mixer's PART page, in semitones (2 by default).

Which MIDI messages work? Notes, pitch bend, the sustain pedal (CC 64) and other CCs, on the DIN input. Each Sound carries a MIDI map of up to 16 CCs, per Part: SETTINGS › PART › MIDI MAP. To learn a CC, touch a cell, hold MIX and press SEQ, then move the knob on your controller. USB doesn't carry MIDI.

I hear nothing from a Part. Check its CH matches your keyboard, its LEVEL is up, and its OUT. A Part sent to output pair P2 or P3 is silent on the main pair: the header then shows OUT P2 or OUT P3.

How do I tune the whole synth? SETTINGS › AUDIO ROUTING › TUNING.

Effects

How do the effects work? Each Part sends to three shared effects. Open the mixer (Bn twice, or MIX + Bn) and use SND: the CHORUS, DELAY and REVERB send levels. The same page is AMP › SENDS on the Part's chain (SEQ from AMP). PLUS walks on to the effects themselves (annotated in the quick start):

PageCellsSub-page
CHR (chorus)MODE (OFF, I, II, I+II), RATE, DEPTH, MIX
DLY (delay)TYPE (TAPE, GRAIN), TIME, FDBK, TONE, REV (delay into the reverb), MIXCHARACTER: MECH (wow and flutter), SAT, for TAPE. GRAIN: SIZE, DENSITY, PITCH, TEXTURE, for GRAIN
REV (reverb)TYPE (QUAD, ROOM, HALL, BLOOM, GATE, REVERSE), SIZE, TIME, DAMP, SMEAR, MIXMORE, MORE 2: extra controls for the TYPE
MST (master)Compressor: THRESH, RATIO, ATK, REL, MAKEUP, MIXLVL: VOL and PAN of the whole synth

TAPE is a tape echo. GRAIN, in the manner of Mutable Instruments' Beads, builds its repeats from short grains of the last half second: SIZE is a grain's length, DENSITY how many start each second, PITCH shifts each grain (+12 with a high FDBK is a shimmer), and TEXTURE scatters where and when they start. CHARACTER's cells are heard under TAPE and GRAIN's under GRAIN; under the other TYPE they are dimmed.

A Sound keeps its send levels when you save it.

The screen

How do I turn the hints off? SETTINGS › PERSONALIZE › THEME › HINTS: OFF. The combos still work.

How do I switch to light mode? SETTINGS › PERSONALIZE › THEME › INVERT: ON. The same page has BRIGHT, GAMMA, ACCENT and BLACK. These settings belong to the unit, not to a project.

Can I change the screensaver? SETTINGS › PERSONALIZE › SCREENSAVER: OFF, or from 5 seconds up to 10 minutes. The default is 5 minutes. A button that wakes the screen also does its job. A turn that wakes it loses its first notch, so you never change a value you couldn't see.

Can I have the sub-pages as a vertical list? SETTINGS › PERSONALIZE › CHAIN MAP › PAGES: STACK. With STACK, EDIT goes down a sub-page and SEQ goes back up. With ROW (the default), the sub-pages sit in a row: EDIT goes back one and SEQ goes on one.

What do EDIT and MENU do in a dialog? EDIT is OK. MENU or SEQ cancels. Any encoder moves the highlight. The hint under the question reads EDIT OK · MENU BACK. The risky ones (DELETE, CLEAR, SAVE OVER) open on CANCEL, so a quick EDIT does no harm.

Why is a WAVE or ALG choice dimmed? The formant budget can't pay for it. The focus band says why, for example OP 1 MODULATED · OVER BUDGET or BUDGET FULL · OTHER FORMANTS. Nothing is changed until you land on a choice that isn't dimmed.

The FREQ number doesn't move when an LFO moves the sound. That's on purpose. Every number shows the value you set (FREQ, or CUTOFF on the 2040, included). On the FILTER page, modulation is a faint band under the picture, from the cutoff you set to where it is now.

Can I make the text bigger? SETTINGS › PERSONALIZE › TEXT SIZE › SIZE: LARGE. The cell labels and values, the focus label, the hints, the SETTINGS lists and the Sound names go up a size where they fit. The header, the chain map, the big value and the pictures stay as they are.

What does MIX + B1-6 PART mean in the mixer? Hold MIX and press B1 to B6 to jump to that Part's mixer. You stay on the same page.

Some SETTINGS rows are grey. They say LATER and aren't built yet; EDIT skips them.

Firmware and bugs

Which version am I running? It's on the boot splash, and at SETTINGS › SYSTEM › ABOUT.

Which file do I flash? CHIMERA.BIN, from the Releases page. Its partner CHIMERA.BK2 goes onto the card beside it: Chimera needs both, and installs CHIMERA.BK2 by itself on the first start (DON'T POWER OFF on the screen). The file names never carry a version; the version is in the release title and on the boot splash.

How do I update the firmware, or go back to stock? Follow the install guide: the same steps install a new version over an old one. Copy the new CHIMERA.BIN, CHIMERA.BK2 and factory set onto the card, flash CHIMERA.BIN, and let Chimera install CHIMERA.BK2. Your projects and Sounds on the card stay. For the USB method, SETTINGS › SYSTEM › OS UPGRADE restarts the unit into DFU mode. To return to the stock firmware: Switching back to the original PreenFM3 firmware.

The bootloader lists CHIMER~1.BIN. Which one do I pick? On a Mac, copying CHIMERA.BIN also leaves a hidden twin called ._CHIMERA.BIN, and the bootloader shows it as CHIMER~1.BIN. It isn't firmware. Press − / + until the list shows CHIMERA.BIN, the big one, and flash that. Copying from Windows or Linux avoids the twin.

My browser saved CHIMERA (1).BIN. That's a second download of the same file. Rename it back to CHIMERA.BIN in Finder or Explorer (and CHIMERA (1).BK2 to CHIMERA.BK2). Rename, don't delete. When you copy it to the card, choose Replace.

TAPE, ORBIT, the reverb or something else is silent and says UPDATE or NEEDS UPDATE. CHIMERA.BK2 isn't installed, or it's from another version. Copy the CHIMERA.BK2 from the same release as your CHIMERA.BIN to the top of the card (hold MENU at power-on, then button 3), eject, and restart. The screen says DON'T POWER OFF while it installs.

How do I report a bug? Check KNOWN-ISSUES.md first. Then Issues › New issue › Bug report, with the version, what you did and what you heard or saw. A photo, screenshot or short recording helps a lot. See reporting bugs.

I hear a hum when the unit is powered over USB. That's a ground loop with your computer, not a bug. Use the 9 V supply. See KNOWN-ISSUES.md.
