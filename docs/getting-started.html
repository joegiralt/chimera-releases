Getting started with Chimera

A short tour for alpha testers. To install (or go back to stock), follow the install guide; this guide starts once Chimera boots. The quick start explains the screen, the controls and every page with annotated screenshots; the FAQ answers "how do I…".

You will need a MIDI keyboard or sequencer on the DIN MIDI input. USB doesn't carry MIDI.

First five minutes

  1. Power on. The boot splash shows the firmware version; note it, you will want it for bug reports. (It's also at SETTINGS › SYSTEM › ABOUT.)
  2. Press B1. That is Part 1. There are six Parts (B1 to B6), each a separate instrument. The six dots at the top of the screen are the Parts: the current one is a bright green dot, and a Part that is playing gets a ring.
  3. Play Part 1 from your keyboard. Out of the box, Part n listens on MIDI channel n, is POLY, and plays out of output pair P1.
  4. Turn the six encoders under the screen. Each one drives the cell above it.
  5. Choose what a Part is: hold EDIT and press B1 to open the Sound browser. The top rows are INIT sounds, one per engine (ALGO, MODAL, VA, TAPE), then DRUM, which says COMING SOON and doesn't load yet, above the project's Sounds; each row names its engine. The list opens on Sound 1, so turn encoder 1 up for INIT, to the one you want, and press EDIT to load it. Turn encoder 2 for the LIBRARY and FACTORY tabs: FACTORY holds the 128 factory Sounds once they are on the card.

Getting around

Every Part is a chain of pages. MINUS / PLUS move along it. A page's sub-pages sit in a row at the bottom of the screen: SEQ goes on to the next one (wrapping round at the end) and EDIT goes back one. The same keys work everywhere: Parts, the mixer, the Sound browser, ORBIT and SETTINGS. In a list, any encoder moves the bar. In a dialog, EDIT is OK and MENU or SEQ cancels. Touch a cell and the focus band shows any combo it answers to. The quick start has the whole map, the key combos and an annotated tour of the screen.

Parts and engines

Each Part runs one engine. Load a Sound with another engine and the Part's pages change to match. Every engine's chain ends in the same FLT, AMP and MOD pages.

EngineWhat it isPages
ALGOsix-operator phase-modulation synthALG (and PITCH), OSC (and its per-operator sub-pages), FLT, AMP, MOD
MODALstruck, plucked and bowed resonatorsEXC (exciter), RES (models STRING, BANK, BOWED, SYMP; and PITCH), FLT, AMP, MOD
VAtwo variable-wave oscillators combined by MODE, with a SUB, a NOISE source and STABILITY driftVA (and its OSC1, OSC2, NOISE and PITCH sub-pages), FLT, AMP, MOD
TAPEplays sound sources from the card through a machine: WALK, STRETCH, SCRUB or WAVESRC (the source), DECK (and the machine's own page and PITCH), FLT, AMP, MOD

FLT holds the filter (CLEAN, 2040, DIRTY, LADDER or SEM) and the voice's FX slot; AMP holds the VCA, its low-pass gate and the Part's effect sends. Filters, modulation and the effects are in the quick start and the FAQ.

Voices and CPU

Chimera has 8 voices in all, shared by the six Parts. Each note costs a share of the processor that depends on its Sound: the engine, the model, the filter and the FX slot. A note gets a voice only if its cost fits in what is left, so a light Sound plays all 8 and a heavy one fewer.

The delay's GRAIN type and the ROOM, HALL and BLOOM reverbs cost about a voice: with one of them on, many Sounds play one voice fewer.

These counts are for the rev V chip (480 MHz). A rev Y chip runs at 400 MHz and has less to share, so heavier Sounds play fewer voices. SETTINGS › SYSTEM › ABOUT shows which chip yours has.

How do I...

Load and save Sounds and projects, route modulation, set MIDI channels, glide and bend, use TAPE and the effects, change the theme, update the firmware or report a bug: see the FAQ.

Not working yet

  • MIDI input is DIN only.
  • DRUM isn't in this release.
  • SETTINGS rows that are dimmed are not built yet; EDIT skips them.

Alpha notes

  • Check KNOWN-ISSUES.md before filing.
  • Chimera reads and writes only under /CHIMERA/ on the card, and reads CHIMERA.BK2 from the card's main folder when it installs. Your stock PreenFM3 files are untouched.
  • Reporting a bug: Issues › New issue › Bug report. Give the version (boot splash or ABOUT), what you did, and what you heard or saw. A short recording or photo helps.
